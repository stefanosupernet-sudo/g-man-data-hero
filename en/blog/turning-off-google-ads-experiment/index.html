THE VERDICT

€77,000 less in sales to save €1,880. You cut waste — not the engine that drives revenue.

When performance looks expensive, the temptation is to pause campaigns. This real e-commerce test shows what happens when Google Ads is switched off for a month: a tiny media saving and a massive drop in conversion value.

What the data showed

  • Conversion value collapsed by about €77,600.
  • ROAS dropped from 25.69 to 9.37 (−63%).
  • Media spend fell only by €1,880 — a false economy.

Attribution is imperfect, but the order of magnitude is clear: Google Ads was funding demand, not only burning budget.

What to do instead

Audit structure, tracking and bid strategy. Fix broken conversion signals. Isolate wasteful queries and SKUs. Do not turn off the channel that prints revenue.

FAQ

Should I turn off Google Ads to save money?

Only after a structured audit. Pausing a profitable channel often costs more in lost sales than it saves in media spend, as this experiment shows.

What is a safer alternative?

Tighten structure, fix tracking, cut wasteful segments and reallocate budget — not a full stop.
