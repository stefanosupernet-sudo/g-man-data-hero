Custom Labels: Filter Products by Competition

When hundreds of SKUs are priced out of market, Shopping and Performance Max keep spending on products that cannot convert. Custom labels fix that at feed level.

The method

  • Build or buy a price benchmark versus the market
  • Map SKUs into tiers via custom_label (competitive / borderline / out-of-market)
  • Use a supplemental feed to keep labels updated
  • Structure campaigns or PMax asset groups to exclude or deprioritise hopeless SKUs

Budget concentrates on products that can actually sell — not on catalogue noise.

FAQ

What is a custom_label in Merchant Center?

A free-form product attribute (up to five labels) you define to segment the catalogue for campaigns and reporting.

Does this work with Performance Max?

Yes — segmenting the feed improves what PMax can prioritise when combined with listing groups or feed rules.
