PrestaShop Ghost Tracking: Anatomy of a Data Disaster

Missing purchases, phantom conversions and contradictory reports. When PrestaShop modules, cache and GTM collide, Google Ads optimises noise instead of real sales.

Why PrestaShop tracking breaks

Third-party modules and aggressive cache layers make tags fire twice, not at all, or only on some browsers. Consent Mode and enhanced conversions amplify mistakes if the data layer is inconsistent.

Typical symptoms

  • Google Ads conversion counts ≠ Analytics e-commerce transactions
  • Smart Bidding learns on duplicated or missing purchase events
  • ROAS swings that track tag changes more than real sales

The fix

Map every tag and module. Strip redundant pixels. Rebuild a clean data layer for purchase and key funnel steps. Align Google Ads primary conversions with one trusted source of truth — then re-enable value-based bidding.

FAQ

Why does PrestaShop struggle more than Shopify on tracking?

Heavy module ecosystems and cache layers often inject or block tags unpredictably.

Should I trust Google Ads or Analytics numbers?

Neither blindly. Align both to a single clean purchase event and reconcile with the order backend.
