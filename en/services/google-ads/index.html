What I do in practice

  • Tracking & conversion audit — GTM, GA4, enhanced conversions
  • Account structure — Search, Shopping, PMax, Demand Gen by goal and margin
  • Feed & Merchant Center — clean catalogue, custom labels
  • Audiences & remarketing — lists from real events
  • Tests & reporting — experiments and dashboards that explain why

Bid strategies I use most

  • Maximize clicks — traffic volume; useful while gathering data
  • Maximize conversions — volume within budget; needs solid tracking
  • Maximize conversion value — prefers higher-value outcomes
  • Target ROAS — aims at a return (e.g. 400%); needs value history
  • Target CPA — fixed cost per acquisition; good for similar-value leads/sales

No strategy replaces clean tracking. If conversions are duplicated or incomplete, Smart Bidding flies blind.
