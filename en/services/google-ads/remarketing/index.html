🔄 REMARKETING

Talk again to people who already engaged — with lists built on real behaviour.

Remarketing only works if lists are based on clean events and sensible membership windows.

  • Lists from GA4 audiences or Ads tags
  • Exclude converters where appropriate
  • Creative that acknowledges prior visit

Why use it: efficiency on warm traffic. When not: tiny traffic, broken pixels, or privacy settings that empty the lists.
