Google Ads · Shopping

🛒 SHOPPING ON GOOGLE ADS

Product listings driven by your Merchant Center feed — not by keyword poetry.

Shopping shows products (image, price, title) from your feed. Quality lives or dies in Merchant Center.

  • Clean titles, GTIN, price and availability
  • Custom labels for margin and priority
  • Disapproved products fixed before scaling spend

Why use it: commercial intent + visual proof. When not: thin or non-compliant catalogue, or services with no product feed.

Often runs alongside Performance Max; the feed still has to be truthful.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Shopping right for us?

If you sell products with a solid feed, usually yes.

Can it run alone?

Yes for many retailers; often stronger with Search and PMax.

What must be ready first?

A clean feed and conversion tracking.

How do you measure success?

ROAS and margin by product segment, not only clicks.

Do you fix Merchant Center too?

Yes — feed quality is part of the job.
