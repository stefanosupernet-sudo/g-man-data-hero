Google Ads · Performance Max

⚡ PERFORMANCE MAX

Automation across Search, Shopping, Display, YouTube and more — only as smart as your data.

Performance Max is Google’s multi-channel automation. It needs conversion value, a healthy feed (if retail), and clear guardrails.

  • Clean conversion signals
  • Asset groups with real creative discipline
  • Brand exclusions and URL controls where needed
  • Human review of search themes and placement waste

Why use it: scale when data is trustworthy. When not: broken tracking, chaotic feed, or brand-safety constraints you can’t encode.

Frequently asked questions

Is PMax right for us?

After tracking and feed are healthy — often yes for scale.

Replace Search?

Not always; many accounts keep branded Search separate.

What must be ready first?

Reliable conversions and, for retail, a clean feed.

How do you measure success?

Business ROAS/CPA, not only in-channel metrics.

Do you monitor waste?

Yes — search themes, placements and brand controls.
