Google Ads · Search

🔍 SEARCH ON GOOGLE ADS

High-intent keyword campaigns for people already looking for what you sell.

Search is the shop window for demand that already exists. Someone types a query; you answer with an ad and a landing page that matches.

  • Keyword structure and match types with discipline
  • Negatives to cut waste
  • Ad copy aligned to intent and landing
  • Conversion tracking before aggressive Smart Bidding

Why use it: clearest intent signal in the Google ecosystem. When not to lean only on it: if the brand is unknown and you need upper-funnel volume, pair with Demand Gen or YouTube — not Search alone as a brand factory.

Depends on solid tracking and often on GA4 audiences for observation.
